Pillars of Practice

How I approach Digital Media Education

Structured principles designed to guide creative discovery and technological fluency.

01
Hands-On Creation

Active Multimedia Production

From vector illustrations and digital graphics to video and sound editing, students learn best by building real artifacts they can take pride in sharing.

  • Project-based design exercises with real-world briefs
  • Foundational art & design principles applied to digital mediums
02
Web & Code as Medium

Interactive Web Storytelling

Teaching the web as a dynamic storytelling environment. Demystifying code (HTML, CSS, Git) so students understand the digital architecture shaping their everyday lives.

  • Accessible website creation with GitHub Pages & modern styling
  • Visual hierarchy, user experience (UX), and responsive layouts
03
Lifelong Fluency

Empowerment & Critical Digital Literacy

Equipping learners with the analytical tools to evaluate digital information, celebrate diverse perspectives, and navigate evolving creative technologies with confidence.

  • Constructive peer feedback frameworks and critique culture
  • Ethical considerations in digital sharing and creative ownership
